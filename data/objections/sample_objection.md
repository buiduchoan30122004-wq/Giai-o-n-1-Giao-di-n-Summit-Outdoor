# Customer Objections

## Objection: Price is too high
**Context**: Customer likes the shoe but feels it is out of their budget.
**Response Strategy**: Highlight the durability and longevity of the shoe. A higher upfront cost pays off because they won't need to replace them as often as cheaper alternatives. Offer a first-time buyer discount if applicable.

## Objection: Too heavy
**Context**: Customer is used to road running shoes and finds these bulky.
**Response Strategy**: Explain that the extra weight comes from the rock plate and reinforced toe cap, which are essential for protection on technical trails.
